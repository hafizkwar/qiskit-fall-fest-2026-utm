About the fall fest

Building UTM's quantum future.

Qiskit Fall Fest brings computing and physics students into one room to understand, build, share, and create a community that outlives a single event.

See the programmeRegistration status

One state · many possible futures

Strategic collaboration

Quantum grows between disciplines.

The event is organised by students in collaboration with IBM Quantum, UTM's Faculty of Computing, and Faculty of Science. The shared goal is a beginner-friendly path from concept to circuit.

FACULTY OF SCIENCE

Understand the state

Build intuition for the physics beneath qubits, measurement, superposition, and entanglement.

FACULTY OF COMPUTING

Program the circuit

Connect the idea to code through practical Qiskit sessions and a cloud quantum workflow.

IBM QUANTUM

Reach real systems

Join a global learning programme built around open tools, cloud access, and shared exploration.

STUDENT COMMITTEE

Keep it moving

Create an annual handover and a welcoming place for the next cohort to learn together.

Core objectives

Learn. Build. Belong.

01

Make quantum approachable

Give beginners enough physical intuition and computing context to take a confident first step.

02

Turn theory into practice

Use guided Qiskit sessions so attendees leave having worked with real circuit concepts.

03

Launch lasting continuity

Start the UTM Quantum Community, build a path to MyQI affiliation, and support annual student leadership.
